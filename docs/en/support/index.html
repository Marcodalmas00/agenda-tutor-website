Contacting support

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and app version or build. Describe the steps, what you expected and what happened. If you attach images, use fictional examples or hide real student names, contacts, notes and amounts.

Do not send passwords, Apple or bank credentials, complete agenda backups or personal documents. A small example is enough to explain the issue.

TestFlight beta access

The beta is private and requires a TestFlight invitation. Follow the invitation and Apple’s instructions; this website does not provide a public download. You can send feedback through TestFlight or email without including real student data.

Demo mode uses an agenda separate from your real one. Changes to demo data do not change your teaching database.

Add to Calendar

Open a scheduled lesson and choose Add to Calendar. Apple’s editor proposes the title, location, times and an alert 30 minutes before. Select your calendar, including iCloud if already available on the device, then edit and save or cancel.

This is an independent copy. If you move or cancel the lesson in Agenda Tutor, also update the Calendar event. Adding it again creates another event. The event alert is separate from the app’s optional local reminder, which is scheduled 15 minutes before a lesson.

Backups and restoring

In Settings, export a complete backup and keep it in Files or another destination you choose. It includes students, personal notes, lessons, the payment ledger and attachments. The app does not add a password to the file.

Restore shows a preview and replaces the database after confirmation, keeping a local recovery copy of the previous data. Before changing devices or deleting the app, also save a copy outside the app. Apple device backup is a separate service; the agenda does not have its own iCloud synchronization.

Notes, materials and receipts

Student notes in a student’s details brings together teaching notes, next steps and optional personal context. Study materials can be files or images selected with iPhone’s pickers. In the edit form, additions and removals take effect when you save; Cancel discards the draft.

Receipts and payment notes belong to the corresponding payment. Adding or removing them does not change its recorded amount. Removing an attachment from the app does not delete the original in your library or selected provider.

Payments, Free and Pro

Record payment logs money you have already received. The app does not request transfers, move funds or access your Hype, Revolut or bank account. Money received above the outstanding balance becomes that student’s credit.

Free allows up to three active students. Pro is intended to extend limits and some features. Products and purchases may not yet be available during the beta; refer to the options shown in the app and Apple’s interface. This website does not sell subscriptions or publish prices.
