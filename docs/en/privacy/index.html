Who handles the data

The developer is Marco Dal Mas. Support and privacy contact: dev@marcodalmas00.com. Marco Dal Mas is the controller of data received to handle support requests and feedback.

The tutor decides which student information to enter in the agenda and for what purposes, and is responsible for its use according to their role and applicable law. The app does not send its database to the developer.

The database on your iPhone

Agenda Tutor stores students and payers, manually entered contacts, subjects, lessons, attendance, teaching notes, next steps, optional personal-context notes, recorded payment amounts, methods and dates, packages, refunds and selected attachments locally.

No app account is required. This version has no developer-operated backend, advertising, developer analytics or tracking, and does not synchronize its own database with iCloud. The payment ledger does not transfer money or obtain bank credentials or card details to pay for lessons.

Photos, files and personal notes

iPhone’s pickers let you select individual files or images to copy into the app as materials or receipts. The app does not inspect your entire photo library, access your address book or use the camera for this feature.

Student preferences, sports, interests, difficulties and background are optional fields. Enter only information relevant to your teaching relationship.

A copy in Calendar

Add to Calendar opens Apple’s editor with participant names, the subject if present, location, start, end, time zone and an initial alert 30 minutes before. You can edit the proposal, select a calendar, and save or cancel. Teaching notes, personal-context notes and payments are not copied.

The app does not request full access or read existing events. If you select an iCloud calendar available on your device, Apple may synchronize that copy according to your settings. Later moves and cancellations in the app do not update the copy; adding it again creates another event.

Sharing and backups

You choose the recipient, app or provider for reports, prepared reminders and backups. A complete backup includes personal notes and attachments. PDF and CSV reports exclude the five personal-context fields, but may contain names, teaching notes and amounts. Check the content before sharing it.

The app does not add a password to exported backups. Keep copies appropriately at your chosen destination. Separately, Apple device backups may include the app’s data according to your settings; this is not iCloud synchronization of the agenda.

Reminders and Apple services

The app’s local reminders are optional and request permission when enabled. They may display a name and subject 15 minutes before the lesson. You can disable them and control notification previews in iPhone Settings. They are separate from Calendar event alerts.

Any Pro purchases use StoreKit and Apple services. The app verifies entitlements on the device; the developer does not receive your Apple payment card details through the app.

TestFlight beta and feedback

Apple may make beta diagnostics and tester-submitted feedback available to the developer, including shared text and screenshots. This is separate from analytics implemented in the app. Use fictional examples when reporting issues and avoid images containing real student information.

Support messages and retention

When you contact support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to handle your request and reply, retaining them for as long as needed for this purpose, related follow-up and applicable obligations.

Where the GDPR applies, responding to requests and handling reported issues is based on the legitimate interest in providing support. Where necessary for a requested contractual service or legal obligations, those respective legal bases apply. Email is also handled through the relevant mail provider.

Deleting data and external copies

Archiving a student keeps their lessons and financial history. To remove the entire local database, use Delete App and its associated data on iPhone. Removing only the icon or freeing storage while keeping app data does not delete the database.

Device backups, Calendar events, exported files and copies sent to others remain independent; manage them at their respective destinations. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase a local database that they do not receive.

This website and other providers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record IP addresses and technical information to operate and secure the service; these logs are not developer analytics. The website does not integrate its own profiling or advertising tools.

Apple, GitHub, email providers and your chosen sharing destinations apply their own privacy notices, including for processing in countries other than yours.

Requests and rights

For data received by the developer, contact dev@marcodalmas00.com to request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability where those rights apply. You may complain to the competent supervisory authority, including Italy’s Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

For information held in a tutor’s agenda, contact the tutor managing it. Changes to the app’s or website’s data flows will be described in an updated notice.
